MVP is an edited real human announcer over an actual crowd recording. It is **not an unedited live-stadium MVP chant**. The source prohibits AI voice replication; no AI voice replication was used. These are conventional crop, repeat, resample, delay, echo and mix edits of the licensed recording. Credit the voice as “MVP voice: VoiceBosch / SoundBiterSFX, Announcer Pack – Grenadier, CC BY 4.0; edited and mixed with crowd.”

All original source licenses were verified from their Freesound pages during preparation. Files named *-source.mp3 and prepare.py are working materials and do not need shipping. Ship this attribution file or an equivalent visible Credits panel with the game.

Validation: ffprobe confirms all seven game MP3s are decodable with durations 0.470 / 0.679 / 0.758 / 0.549 / 3.448 / 3.631 / 5.538 seconds respectively. Distinctive-content identification for impact/MVP is based on the authors’ recording description and numbered track list; no speech-recognition check was available.
